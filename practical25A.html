About Us

S.E.S. Polytechnic, Solapur is a reputed technical institute established in 1983, run by the Solapur Education Society. Located in the heart of Solapur, Maharashtra, the institute offers diploma programs in various engineering disciplines.

The institute is affiliated with the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai and approved by AICTE, New Delhi. With modern laboratories, experienced faculty, and strong industry connections, SES Polytechnic provides quality technical education to students from Solapur and surrounding regions.

Our focus is on practical learning, industry exposure, and overall student development — producing graduates who are job-ready from day one.
