Who We Are

A Legacy of Technical Excellence

S.E.S. Polytechnic, Solapur is an esteemed technical institution affiliated with the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE). Founded with a vision to nurture skilled technical professionals, the institute has grown to become a cornerstone of engineering education in the Solapur district.

The institution is run under the Solapur Education Society, a trust committed to holistic education and social upliftment. With state-of-the-art laboratories, an experienced faculty, and an industry-integrated curriculum, SES Polytechnic continues to produce graduates who excel in both corporate environments and entrepreneurial pursuits.

Our approach is grounded in hands-on learning, practical exposure, and character development — preparing students not just for careers, but for life.
